# Glossary and abbreviations (PoE 1)

## Modes and context

- **SC / HC / SSF / HCSSF**: Softcore, Hardcore, Solo Self-Found, Hardcore SSF. In 3.30, Hardcore leagues are parented to Standard: a dead character moves to Standard, not to the challenge league's softcore.
- **Ruthless**: separate, harsher ruleset with its own notes. Never mix Ruthless changes into normal-mode advice.
- **PoE 2**: a separate game with separate trees, gems and economy. PoE 2 1.0 is planned for 11 December 2026. Sites have PoE 2 sections (poe2db, poe.ninja/poe2, Craft of Exile PoE 2 mode); ignore them for PoE 1 questions.
- **League start**: the first days of a league, on fresh characters with no currency.
- **Starter**: a build that can do the campaign and early maps on cheap or self-found gear.

## Classes and ascendancies (3.29 tree, PoB snapshot)

Classes: Marauder, Duelist, Ranger, Shadow, Witch, Templar, Scion.

Ascendancies:
- Juggernaut, Berserker, Chieftain (Marauder)
- Slayer, Gladiator, Champion (Duelist)
- Deadeye, Warden, Pathfinder (Ranger)
- Assassin, Saboteur, Trickster (Shadow)
- Necromancer, Occultist, Elementalist (Witch)
- Inquisitor, Hierophant, Guardian (Templar)
- Ascendant (Scion)
- Reliquarian and Luminary (newer ascendancies)
- Event or alternate entries in PoB's tree: Warden of the Maji, Warlock of the Mists, Wildwood Primalist.

**Bloodlines** are secondary ascendancy-like choices: Abyssal, Aul, Breachlord, Catarina, Chaos, Delirious, Farrul, Lycia, Nameless, Olroth, Oshabi, Saresh, Velka. Confirm which remain active in 3.30 before recommending one.

## Damage types and terms

- **Hit** versus **DoT**; **ailment** versus **debuff**; **increased** (additive) versus **more** (multiplicative); **added damage** versus **base damage**.
- **Penetration** only applies to hits. **Exposure** and curses lower resistance for all damage.
- **Overkill**: damage beyond a monster's remaining life.
- **Ailment threshold**: the value that controls the magnitude of shock, chill and similar effects.
