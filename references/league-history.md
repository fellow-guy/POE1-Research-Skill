## 3.30 launch

- Patch notes: https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4013680. Posted 2026-11-10 by Stacey_GGG.
- Launch: Friday, 13 November 2026, US Pacific afternoon. Third-party sites gave "1 PM PDT" or "1 PM CT", which conflict. In mid-November, Pacific time is PST (UTC−8). Confirm on the official countdown.
- Path of Exile 2 1.0 is planned for 11 December 2026 (per the Massively OP headline, 2026-11-09).

## Using history

- To trace a mechanic, start with the league that introduced it, then the leagues that made it core or reworked it. Read those patch notes selectively.
- Mechanics that became core change behaviour when core. Mirage became core in 3.30, for example.
- Patch notes live in the [Patch Notes forum](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-forum/patch-notes). Each major version has a main thread plus hotfix threads (3.30.0b, c, and so on). Hotfixes often change new mechanics in the first two weeks.
