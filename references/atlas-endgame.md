# Atlas and endgame (PoE 1)

This file records the 3.30 endgame state plus stable concepts. The Atlas is reworked often, so check patch notes and the [official Atlas tree](https://www.pathofexile.com/atlas-skill-tree) before giving a farming plan.

## Progression skeleton

1. **Campaign → maps** at about level 68–70. Tiers T1–T16 are area levels 68–83. Atlas completion and map bosses give Atlas passive points.
2. **Voidstones**: obtained from the Searing Exarch, Eater of Worlds, Maven and Uber Elder. Socketing them raises map tiers in each quadrant. With four Voidstones, everything drops as T16.
3. **Pinnacle bosses**: Shaper, Elder, Uber Elder, Sirus, Maven, Searing Exarch, Eater of Worlds, and more.
   - 3.30 lists Fractal Gargantuan, Fractal Titan, Synthete Masterpiece and Synthete Nightmare from Cortex as Pinnacle Atlas Bosses.
   - Atziri moved into the Temple of Atzoatl (Cradle of Creation, area level 84) in 3.30.
4. **T17 / Valdo / Voidborn Reliquary** content is high-end and has hard modifiers. Starter guides often exclude it.

## 3.30 Atlas changes that change farming

- **Atlas tree**: completely reworked. It starts in the centre and grows outward, with 150 points (was 138).
  - Maven, Exarch, Eater and the six special invitations give 2 skill + 2 respec points. Uber Elder gives 4 skill + 2 respec.
  - Delve and Heist clusters are removed.
  - Tertiary mechanics (Beyond, Buried Sentinels, Delirium Mirrors, Mirage, Viridian Wildwood) only have frequency clusters.
  - Generic clusters exist: Magic, Rare and Unique monsters; Divination Cards; Gold; Maps.
- **Keystones**:
  - New: **Phantom Anomalies**.
  - Removed: Crop Rotation, Endless Tide, Lucid Dreams, Meticulous Appraiser, Refiner's Bargain, Synthesised Stability.
  - Reworked: **Unwavering Vision** — only one Thread type; 75% of Threads become Gold; +2 Threads beyond the cap.
  - Changed: **Speaker of the Dead** — 50 possessions; 30-second player possession.
- **Threads replace Scarabs**. There are 10 types: Abyss, Bestiary, Betrayal, Blight, Breach, Expedition, Incursion, Legion, Ritual and Ultimatum.
  - Up to 8 per map. More Threads add increasingly likely transformative effects.
  - **Frayed Threads** give a random Thread. Scarabs converted on login.
  - Thread sources: altars, strongboxes, Tormented Heretics and Maven's Chisel of Threads.
- **Shaped Regions**: 10 types, one per Thread type. They give free Threads to their maps; completing the objective adds 1 free Thread to other maps in the region. Regenerate them from the Atlas centre when you have fewer than 10.
  - **Astrolabes** apply a specific Shaped Region to a chosen map.
- **Atlas Drops replaces map Item Quantity**. Item rarity now drives currency quality, divination card rarity, Item Tier and Unique upgrades.
  - Map rarity sources were cut: Nightmare/Originator modifiers about −40–50%; Eldritch altar rarity 10–20%; Memory Trace altars 20–25%; Quantity altars removed; Pillars of Arun 100–150%.
  - The "player rarity versus monster rarity" question needs fresh testing this league. Do not reuse 3.29 magic-find conclusions.
- **Delirium Orbs** now reforge Rare maps with a Delirious modifier (5–50% each, same type stacks to 100%). Each 20% adds one extra reward of that type. Mixing types resets it. Mirrors of Delirium were buffed.
- **Mirage is core**. The Black Barya is area level 84; Saresh is much harder. Djinn Coins are gone; Djinn Sigils were reworked.
- **Beyond rework**: a Demonic Evocator at map start; Beyond bosses give 60 s of Demonic Power and can open a Demonic realm.
- **Harvest is in-map**: Sacred Flora and Fauna, Wildwood Wisps; a Sacred Specimen spawns a boss in high tiers.
- **Incursion and Temple**: Atziri lives in the Temple. The **Royal Visage** enchants T16s with Alluring Temporal Incursions. Vaal side areas are removed from maps.
- **Map pool rotated**: 31 maps re-added (Academy, Dungeon, Overgrown Ruin, Underground Sea and others) and 31 removed (Alleyways, Toxic Sewer, Waterways and others). See the patch digest for the full list.
- **Map corruption** now adds 2 explicit modifiers (maximum 9) instead of rerolling. This also applies to Logbooks, Contracts, Blueprints and Invitations.
- **Allflame Embers** are currency that enchants maps (new: Cloister, Divine, Multitude). **Divine Vessels** stack to 20 and apply Deified to maps (tougher bosses, 50% boss rarity).
- **Heist**: Whakano sells Contracts and Blueprints for Gold at level 68+. Rogue Markers, Contracts and Blueprints are no longer core drops.
- **Bestiary**: yellow beasts are not captured. Lures do nothing.
- **Ultimatum**: rewards keep improving after round 10.
- **Ritual**: Blood-Filled Vessels need Ritual Threads.

## Farming plan checklist

1. Character capability: clear speed, survivability against the planned map modifiers, and boss damage for Fated/Deified bosses.
2. The Atlas tree and Thread/Shaped Region choices. Record Thread costs versus free Threads.
3. Output measurement: maps per hour, currency per map, sale assumptions, sample size. Start counting after you have enough maps to sustain.
4. Separate "confirmed mechanic" from "profit claim". Early-league profit claims are hypotheses until creators publish repeatable samples.
5. Recheck after hotfixes. GGG often adjusts new Atlas rewards in the first two weeks.
