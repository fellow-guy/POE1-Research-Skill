# Campaign and leveling (PoE 1)

## Stable structure

- **10 acts.** Kitava defeats apply resistance penalties: −30% after Act 5 and −60% total after Act 10 **[PoB `resistancePenalty`]**. Plan to reach 75% elemental resistance for maps after Act 10. Chaos resistance can stay negative early.
- **Passive points**: one per level (99) plus quest rewards. The usual total at level 100 is 123 with "kill all bandits" (+2 points). Siding with a bandit gives stats instead of the points. Use an Exile Leveling route to avoid missing quest points.
- **Labyrinth** gives 2 ascendancy points per lab, 8 in total. Trial locations:
  - **Normal** (Acts 1–3): Lower Prison; Crypt Level 1; Chamber of Sins Level 2; Crematorium; Catacombs; Imperial Gardens.
  - **Cruel** (Acts 6–7): Prison (Act 6); Crypt; Chamber of Sins Level 2 (Act 7).
  - **Merciless** (Acts 8–10): Bath House; Tunnel; Ossuary.
  - **Eternal (Uber)**: six trials found in maps; trial locations show in the Labyrinth trial tracker.
  - Daily layouts: [poelab.com](https://www.poelab.com/).
- **Divine Fonts** in the lab can transfigure gems. That is the SSF route to transfigured gems before drops.
- **Gem access**: quest rewards and Lilly Roth (Act 6+ in town) sell gems. Gems not available to your class's quest reward can be bought from Lilly after Act 6 or dropped. Siosa (after the Act 3 Library quest) sells gems from other classes' reward lists.
  - 3.30: Arcane Link is offered to the Scion for completing Breaking the Seal; Marauder, Duelist and Templar can buy it from Petarus and Vanja.
- **Movement and flasks**: quicksilver flask, a movement skill and life flasks with "of Staunching" or "of Heat" utility matter more than damage while leveling.
- **Vendor routines**: buy linked items and movement-speed boots (magic boots with movement speed from early vendors), and weapon bases with high physical damage or spell damage for casters.
- **Resistance planning**: track resistances at Acts 5 and 10. Bench-craft resistances when short; crafted resistances are cheap on many bases.
- **Gold** (since 3.25): spent on the respec of passives, Kingsmarch, Kirac, gambling, Whakano and other gold vendors. 3.30 lowered mid-campaign gambling cost for non-jewellery.

## 3.30 leveling changes worth noting

- Mid-campaign gambling is cheaper for non-jewellery items.
- Item Tiers appear from Act 6, so more Rare drops have good tiers and links.
- Scrolls of Wisdom sell for 15 Gold at all town vendors.
- Campaign Vaal side-area modifiers now grant rarity instead of quantity.
- Ruthless: league mechanics appear more often in the campaign.
- **Fates on campaign bosses**: each campaign boss can give a distinct Viscera, so a League-start route may include Fating campaign bosses. Weigh added difficulty against reward. This is new: build guides written before launch cannot have tested it.

## Leveling build evaluation

- A good starter names its **leveling skill(s)**, the **swap level**, and the **trees per level band**. Sanavixx's Cyclone export, for example, has separate trees for Lvl 1–12, 12–32, 32, 58, 68, 77 and 90+.
- Check gem colours and links for the leveling setup. Many guides use Spectral Throw, Static Strike, Rolling Magma, Freezing Pulse or Fireball, or totems before the main skill.
- Plan the **lab timing** (normal lab around level 33, cruel around 55–60, merciless around 68–75) and ascendancy order.
- Key early uniques (Tabula Rasa, Goldrim, Wanderlust, Lifesprig and others) are useful but not guaranteed in SSF.
